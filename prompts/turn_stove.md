## Task Specific Tips

To turn on the stove, we need to rotate the black control knob on the back of the stove by 90 degrees counter-clockwise.

Example waypoints:
1. Locate the black control knob on the back of the stove and use `gripper_teleport_via_click` to teleport the gripper on top of the knob.
2. Lower the gripper towards the knob, and adjust the gripper if needed so that the stem of the knob is roughly in the middle of the fingers.
3. Close the gripper to hold the stem of the knob.
4. Rotate the gripper 90 degrees counter-clockwise to turn the stove on.
